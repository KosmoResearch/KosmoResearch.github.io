# Kosmo Research

Official project pages and interactive research demonstrations from Kosmo
Research.

## Projects

### GeoWeaver

**Accurate Long-Sequence 3D Reconstruction via Hierarchical Geometric
Assembly**

GeoWeaver combines chunk-wise geometric priors with hierarchical test-time
adaptation to recover accurate geometry and globally consistent camera
trajectories from long image sequences.

[Explore GeoWeaver →](https://kosmoresearch.github.io/GeoWeaver/)

### UniQuery4R

**Unified 4D Scene Reconstruction from a Single Query**

UniQuery4R is a query-conditioned framework for unified correspondence,
geometry, motion, depth, and camera estimation in dynamic scenes.

[Explore UniQuery4R →](https://kosmoresearch.github.io/UniQuery4R/)

### MVLoMa

**Multi-View Local Feature Matching with Any Geometry Foundation Model**

MVLoMa matches one centre image to all of its neighbours in a single forward
pass, reusing frozen multi-view geometry tokens.

[Explore MVLoMa →](https://kosmoresearch.github.io/MVLoMa/)

### MoRSe

**Moving Regions, Not Moving Objects: Segmenting What Actually Moves under Camera Motion**

MoRSe predicts moving-region masks from RGB frame pairs, distinguishing
actual scene motion from apparent motion induced by the camera.

[Explore MoRSe →](https://kosmoresearch.github.io/MoRSe/)
